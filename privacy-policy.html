CommTrack Privacy Policy

Effective date: [LAUNCH DATE — the day CommTrack goes live on Google Play]

CommTrack is a shop QC tracking app. This policy explains what information CommTrack collects, why, and what we do with it. The short version: we collect the minimum needed to run your account and sync your jobs, we don't sell anything, and you can delete your data.

What we collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address so you can sign in and so shared jobs can identify crew members. Account sign-in is handled by our backend provider, Supabase.

Your job data. The operations lists, checkmarks, timestamps, notes, and group memberships you create or join in CommTrack. This is stored so your shared jobs sync across your devices and with the crew members you share jobs with.

Task photos. Photos you attach to tasks are stored on your device only. They are not uploaded to our servers and do not sync to other devices.

Device permissions. CommTrack asks for camera permission so you can take task photos, and file access so you can import and export job backup files. It does not access your location, contacts, or anything else.

What we do NOT collect. CommTrack contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking. We do not collect your location, contacts, or device identifiers for advertising.

How we use it

  • To run your account and keep you signed in.
  • To sync your shared jobs and groups between your devices and your crew.
  • To keep the service working (debugging, security).

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Where it's stored

Your account and shared job data are stored with Supabase (our backend provider) on servers in the United States. Supabase processes this data only to provide the service — see Supabase's own privacy policy for their practices. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Your choices

  • Export: you can back up all your jobs from inside the app at any time.
  • Delete: group Owners can delete a group, which removes it from every member's device on next sync. To delete your account and all associated data, contact Commtrack.b@gmail.com.
  • Sign out / uninstall: signing out stops syncing. Uninstalling removes local data (including task photos) on that device; your cloud data stays until deleted.

Data retention

We keep your account and shared job data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account or a group, the associated data is deleted and is not retained.

Children's privacy

CommTrack is a professional tool for shop and QC work and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above and post the new version at this URL. Material changes will be flagged in the app.

Contact

Questions about your data: Commtrack.b@gmail.com
