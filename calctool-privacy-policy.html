Calculations Tool Privacy Policy

Effective date: the day Calculations Tool goes live on Google Play.

Calculations Tool is a pair of shop calculators — a vessel calculator and a construction calculator — published by B's Apps. This policy is short because the app does almost nothing with data:

What we collect

Nothing. The app collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, and no analytics or advertising trackers.

Where your data lives

Your calculations exist only inside the app on your device. Nothing is uploaded to our servers or sent anywhere else, and everything is removed when you uninstall the app.

Internet access

The app works fully offline. The only time it touches the internet is if you tap the "CommTrack on Google Play" link, which simply opens that app's page in the Play Store.

Sharing

We sell nothing and share nothing, because there is nothing to sell or share.

Contact

Questions about this policy: Commtrack.b@gmail.com
